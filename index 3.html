My Favourite Recipe: Maggi

About the Recipe

Maggi is my favourite snack. It is tasty and easy to make. I enjoy eating it with my family.

Preparation Time: 2 minutes
Cooking Time: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 packet of Maggi noodles
  • 1 cup of water
  • Maggi masala
  • Chopped onion
  • Chopped tomato
  • Green peas

Cooking Steps

  1. Pour water into a pan.
  2. Bring the water to a boil.
  3. Add the Maggi noodles.
  4. Add the Maggi masala.
  5. Add chopped onion and tomato.
  6. Cook for a few minutes until done.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!

Enjoy your delicious homemade Maggi!
